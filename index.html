How this reading works

This browser demo estimates acne-like redness clusters, dark-spot patches, fine-line texture, and oil/dryness balance from colour and light in the camera image — a lightweight stand-in for the CNN-based detector described in the project this mirror is based on, which would be trained on labelled skin images for higher accuracy. Nothing is uploaded; all of this runs on your device.

Not a medical device and not a dermatological diagnosis. For persistent acne, changing moles, pain, or anything that concerns you, see a dermatologist.
